# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite and volunteered.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory jokes or language, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers at prayagtushar2016@gmail.com. Reports stay
confidential. Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that break
these rules, and may ban repeat offenders from the project.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately.
